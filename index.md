# Webglance — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 September 2026_

Webglance shows a screenshot of a website you choose in a home-screen
widget and refreshes it on a schedule you set. This policy explains what the
app does with information. The short version: **everything stays on your
phone. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising,
and the developer receives nothing.**

## What the app stores, and where

All of the following is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private
storage, and is removed when you delete a widget or uninstall the app:

- **Widget settings** — the website address you enter, the refresh interval,
  resolution, orientation, desktop mode, crop area, and whether you asked to be
  notified of changes.
- **Screenshots** — the most recent capture of each widget's website, and the
  cropped part the widget displays.
- **A change fingerprint** — a very small, low-resolution grey-scale summary of
  the last picture, used only to tell whether the next one is different. It is
  not a picture you could view.
- **Website cookies** — cookies set by the websites you configure (for example,
  when you dismiss a site's cookie banner in the app's built-in browser), so
  those choices are remembered on later refreshes.

None of this is sent to the developer or to any third party by the app.

## What leaves your phone

To take a screenshot, the app loads the website you configured, the same way a
web browser would. That website — and any services the page itself loads —
therefore receives the ordinary information any website visit involves: your
IP address, the device's browser identification (user agent), and any cookies
that site previously set. How those websites handle that information is
governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

The app blocks a small number of advertising and ad-fraud-detection domains
while capturing, for stability reasons. It adds no tracking of its own.

## Notifications

If you turn on "Notify me when the image changes" for a widget, the app
compares each automatic refresh with the previous one **on your device** and
shows a notification if it changed. No notification content is sent anywhere;
there is no push-notification service involved.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — to load the websites you configure.
- **Notifications** — only if you turn on change notifications.
- **Run at startup** — to restart the refresh schedule after the phone reboots.
- **Network state / wake lock** — used by Android's background-work library to
  run refreshes reliably.

The app asks you to set its battery usage to "Unrestricted" so refreshes are
not delayed by battery optimisation. That is a system setting you change
yourself; it gives the app no access to your data.

## Children

The app displays whatever websites the user chooses and is not directed at
children.
